Futures signals

Long and short trades on USDT perpetuals, found by the same 8 strategies (mirrored for shorts) and proven the same way: a strategy may signal only after it made money, after futures fees and funding, on both the older and the newer part of your coins' history. Every plan is sized so the stop loses your risk per trade (1% by default); leverage only sets the margin, and the liquidation price is kept far beyond the stop. Analysis only: you place every order yourself. Leverage magnifies mistakes, so start small.

Track record

What happened after each buy signal, measured on later candles (stop, targets, break-even after TP1, time limit; net of costs). This is the real accuracy of the signals, not a backtest.

Strategy lab

Trains the scalp rules on real history: 48 variants (8 entry filters x 6 exit styles) are backtested on the selected coins. The older 70% of the history chooses the variant; the newer 30%, never used for choosing, checks it. A variant is applied only if it also made money on the newer data. The statistical filter is trained the same way and acts only if it validates.

Evidence engine

Every signal also gets an evidence board: futures positioning (funding, open interest, the crowd's and top traders' long/short ratios, liquidations and an estimated liquidation map), order flow, news and hype (read by the AI if you allow it) and the whole market. It can only hold a buy back, never create one. Setups it holds back are tracked like real trades, so the record shows whether it was right; once enough have closed, a learned model joins the filter, but only if it proves itself on newer outcomes.
